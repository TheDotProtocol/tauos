📜 Terms of Service

Effective Date: December 30, 2024
Last Updated: December 30, 2024

1. Service Description

TauStore provides a sovereign app marketplace for the TauOS ecosystem, offering curated applications with privacy-first principles and zero-knowledge architecture.

2. User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for complying with app terms, not using apps for illegal activities, and respecting intellectual property rights. Developers are responsible for app content and compliance.

3. App Quality & Security

We curate apps for quality and security but do not guarantee their functionality or safety. Users should review app permissions and privacy policies before installation.

4. Payment & Refunds

Paid apps are processed through secure payment systems. Refund policies follow platform standards and developer terms. We do not store payment information.

5. Termination

Either party may terminate service with 30 days notice. Users retain access to purchased apps according to developer terms.

🔒 Privacy Policy

Effective Date: December 30, 2024
Last Updated: December 30, 2024

1. Data Collection

We collect minimal metadata (username, download history, preferences) but never access your personal app data or usage patterns. App data remains private to each application.

2. Data Processing

All app processing occurs within each application's sandbox. We cannot access, read, or analyze your app usage or personal data stored within apps.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except as required by law or with your explicit consent for specific services.

4. Data Retention

Account metadata is retained while your account is active. Download history and preferences can be deleted at any time through your account settings.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your account information. You can export your data and control sharing permissions for app recommendations.

🛡️ Privacy Principles

Our Core Commitments

  • Zero-Knowledge: We cannot access your app data or usage patterns
  • App Sandboxing: Each app operates in isolation with its own privacy controls
  • No Usage Mining: We do not analyze your app behavior or preferences
  • Transparency: Clear app permissions and privacy policies for all applications
  • User Control: You decide what apps to install and what permissions to grant

📊 Transparency Report

🔐 Security Measures

App Verification

All apps undergo security scanning, privacy policy review, and compliance verification before being listed in the store.

Sandboxing

Each app operates in its own isolated environment with strict permission controls and data access limitations.

Audit & Compliance

Regular security audits, penetration testing, and compliance certifications to ensure the highest security standards for the marketplace.

📱 App Developer Guidelines

Privacy Requirements

All apps must have clear privacy policies, minimal data collection, and transparent permission requests. Apps that violate privacy principles are removed.

Security Standards

Apps must pass security scanning, use secure coding practices, and implement proper data protection measures.

Transparency

Developers must clearly communicate what data their apps collect, how it's used, and provide user control over data sharing.
