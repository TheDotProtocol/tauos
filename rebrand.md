## 🎨 **DESIGN SYSTEM**

### **Color Palette**
- **Primary Background**: Dark gradients (#0f0f23, #1a1a2e, #16213e)
- **Accent Color**: Gold (#fbbf24, #f59e0b)
- **Text Colors**: White (#ffffff), Light Gray (#d1d5db), Muted Gray (#9ca3af)
- **UI Elements**: Semi-transparent whites with backdrop blur

### **Typography**
- **Primary Font**: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto
- **Font Weights**: 300 (Light), 400 (Regular), 500 (Medium), 600 (Semi-bold), 700 (Bold), 800 (Extra-bold)

### **Design Elements**
- **Glassmorphism**: Backdrop blur effects with semi-transparent backgrounds
- **Gradients**: Subtle dark gradients for depth and sophistication
- **Shadows**: Soft shadows for depth and hierarchy
- **Border Radius**: Consistent 8px, 12px, 16px, 20px, 24px for modern feel
