🌍 TauOS Sub-processor List

Sovereign Privacy First Operating System

Document Version: 1.0 Date: August 2025

Transparency matters. Below is the current list of third-party providers ("Sub-processors") engaged by Tau LLC to deliver TauOS services.

1. Infrastructure

🏗️ Cloud Provider

TauCloud Infrastructure

Our sovereign cloud infrastructure for hosting and compute resources, built on open-source technologies and operated under strict privacy controls.

⚡ CDN Provider

TauCDN Network

Our distributed content delivery network for performance optimization and global content distribution, ensuring fast and secure access worldwide.

2. Security

🛡️ Monitoring Service

TauGuard Security

Our proprietary intrusion detection and uptime monitoring system, providing 24/7 security oversight and threat detection capabilities.

3. Support & Communication

🎧 Helpdesk Provider

TauSupport Platform

Our integrated user support ticketing system, designed with privacy-first principles and end-to-end encryption for all support communications.

📧 Email Service Provider

TauMail System

Our sovereign email infrastructure for transactional and system emails, featuring end-to-end encryption and zero-knowledge architecture.

4. Principles for Sub-processing

🔒 Our Commitment to Privacy

  • Sub-processors only process data required for their service.
  • Each sub-processor is bound by confidentiality and security obligations.
  • No sub-processor may use data for advertising, profiling, or resale.
  • New sub-processors are announced with 30 days' notice.
  • All sub-processors undergo rigorous security and privacy audits.
  • Data sovereignty is maintained through strict contractual controls.
