## Revenue Clarification (important)
Projections use a *blended device ASP*: direct retail ASP (TauBook/TauPhone) ~ $899–$1,099; OEM-licensed units sold to partners at lower ASP (~$150). Model assumes ~70% OEM-licensed units and 30% retail → blended ASP ≈ **$400**. This accelerates distribution while preserving high-margin direct retail.
