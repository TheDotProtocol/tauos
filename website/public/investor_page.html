The Problem

Large incumbents monetize users' data. AI is cloud-dependent and privacy-invasive. Enterprises need compliant, AI-enabled OS that protects users and scales.

The Solution: TauOS

TauOS is the world's first AI-native, privacy-first hybrid OS across devices and cloud.

Key product pillars:

  • TauAI — Local, privacy-preserving assistant ("Hey Tau").
  • TauVision — On-device visual intelligence.
  • TauMind — Predictive, private personalization.
  • TauSync — Smart file & device sync.
  • TauGuard — AI-first threat protection.
  • TauFlow — Workflow automation & energy optimization.

Product + Device Offering

  • TauOS: OS licensing & enterprise bundles.
  • TauDevices: TauBook (laptop), TauPhone (smartphone), TauPad (tablet) — premium specs, affordable pricing.
  • TauCloud & Enterprise: SaaS subscriptions, BaaS, enterprise integrations.
