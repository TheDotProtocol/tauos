📜 Terms of Service

Effective Date: December 30, 2024
Last Updated: December 30, 2024

1. Service Description

TauBrowser provides a privacy-first web browsing experience with zero-knowledge architecture, built-in ad blocking, and advanced privacy protection features.

2. User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for complying with website terms of service, not using the browser for illegal activities, and respecting intellectual property rights of websites.

3. Privacy Features

We provide privacy tools and features but do not guarantee complete anonymity. Users should understand the limitations of privacy protection and use additional tools as needed.

4. Service Availability

We strive for 99.99% uptime but do not guarantee uninterrupted service. We reserve the right to perform maintenance and updates as needed.

5. Termination

Either party may terminate service with 30 days notice. Upon termination, users must export their browsing data within 90 days.

🔒 Privacy Policy

Effective Date: December 30, 2024
Last Updated: December 30, 2024

1. Data Collection

We collect minimal metadata (username, browser settings) but never access your browsing history, bookmarks, or personal data. All browsing data remains local to your device.

2. Data Processing

All browsing processing occurs client-side. We cannot access, read, or analyze your browsing history, search queries, or personal data stored within the browser.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except as required by law or with your explicit consent for specific services.

4. Data Retention

Account metadata is retained while your account is active. Browsing data is stored locally and can be cleared at any time through browser settings.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your account information. You can export your browsing data and control privacy settings at any time.

🛡️ Privacy Principles

Our Core Commitments

  • Zero-Knowledge: We cannot access your browsing data or history
  • Client-Side Processing: All browsing operations happen on your device
  • No Tracking: We do not track your browsing behavior or preferences
  • Transparency: Open source browser engine and clear privacy controls
  • User Control: You decide what privacy features to enable and what data to share

🔐 Security Measures

Privacy Features

Built-in ad blocking, tracker blocking, fingerprinting protection, and secure browsing modes to protect your privacy while surfing the web.

All local browsing data is encrypted using AES-256 encryption. Your browsing history, bookmarks, and settings are protected from unauthorized access.

Audit & Compliance

Regular security audits, penetration testing, and compliance certifications to ensure the highest security standards for the browser.
