TauID Legal & Privacy Documentation

📜 TauID Terms of Service

Effective Date: December 30, 2024
Last Updated: December 30, 2024

1. Service Description

TauID provides sovereign digital identity management services with zero-knowledge architecture, allowing users to manage their digital identity while maintaining complete control over their personal information.

2. User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their identity credentials, providing accurate information, and not using the service for fraudulent activities or impersonation.

3. Identity Verification

We provide tools for identity verification but do not guarantee the accuracy of user-provided information. Users are responsible for the validity of their identity claims.

4. Service Availability

We strive for 99.99% uptime but do not guarantee uninterrupted service. We reserve the right to perform maintenance and updates as needed.

5. Termination

Either party may terminate service with 30 days notice. Upon termination, users must export their identity data within 90 days.

🔒 TauID Privacy Policy

Effective Date: December 30, 2024
Last Updated: December 30, 2024

1. Data Collection

We collect minimal identity metadata (username, verification status) but never access your encrypted personal information. Your identity keys remain solely in your possession.

2. Data Processing

All identity processing occurs client-side before encryption. We cannot decrypt, read, or analyze your stored personal information.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except as required by law or with your explicit consent for specific services.

4. Data Retention

Account metadata is retained while your account is active. Deleted identity data is permanently removed within 30 days.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your identity information. You can export your data at any time and control sharing permissions.

🛡️ TauID Privacy Principles

Our Core Commitments:

  • Zero-Knowledge: We cannot access your encrypted identity data
  • Client-Side Processing: All identity operations happen on your device
  • No Identity Mining: We do not analyze your identity or behavior patterns
  • Transparency: Open source protocols and regular security audits
  • User Control: You decide what identity information to share and with whom
